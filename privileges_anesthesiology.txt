SPECIALTY: Anesthesiology (Physician)
VERSION: September 23, 2026

SECTION: Anesthesiology
QUALIFICATIONS
Education and Training: Successful completion of an Accreditation Council for Graduate Medical Education (ACGME)- or American Osteopathic Association (AOA)-accredited residency in anesthesiology;
<Blank>
OR An equivalent as set forth in the Medical Staff Bylaws.
Certification: Current certification or board eligible with achievement of certification within 5 years of completing formal training in Anesthesiology by the American Board of Anesthesiology or the American Osteopathic Board of Anesthesiology;
<Blank>
OR An equivalent as set forth in the Medical Staff Bylaws.
<Blank>
AND Current certification in Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support (ACLS) by the American Heart Association;
<Blank>
AND/OR Current certification in Perioperative Resuscitation and Life Support (PeRLS) by the American Society of Anesthesiology.
<Blank>
Maintenance of Certification is required unless a provider is legacy (grandfathered) as set forth in the Medical Staff Bylaws.
New Privilege: Demonstrated current competence with evidence of at least 50 anesthesia encounters within the past 12 months that are reflective of the scope of privileges requested.
<Blank>
OR Successful completion of an ACGME- or AOA-accredited residency, or fellowship training, within the past 12 months.
Renewal of Privilege: Demonstrated current competence with evidence of at least 150 anesthesia encounters within the past 36 months that are reflective of the scope of privileges requested.
PRIVILEGES:
Provide anesthesia care to patients 2 months to less than 24 months of age.
Additional Requirements:
Successful completion of an ACGME- or AOA-accredited fellowship in pediatric anesthesiology;
<Blank>
OR An equivalent as set forth in the Medical Staff Bylaws.
<Blank>
AND Current certification in Pediatric Advanced Life Support (PALS) by The American Heart Association.
Provide anesthesia care to patients 2 years to less than 13 years of age.
Additional Requirements:
Current certification in Pediatric Advanced Life Support (PALS) by The American Heart Association.
Provide anesthesia care to patients 13 years of age and older.
Preoperative evaluation including medical history, physical examination, risk assessment and perioperative planning.
Supervise Certified Registered Nurse Anesthetists (CRNAs).
Administration of monitored anesthesia care, general, neuraxial and regional anesthesia. This includes techniques utilizing intravenous medications, volatile agents, spinal, epidural and peripheral nerve blocks, alone, or in combination.
Monitoring and maintenance of acceptable physiologic parameters during the perioperative period including optimization of neurologic, cardiac, respiratory, hematologic and renal function.
Performance of invasive monitoring techniques including arterial, central venous access procedures.
Use of ultrasound and echocardiography including point-of-care ultrasound as clinically indicated including their use for performance of regional anesthesia, assessment of cardiac and respiratory status, and abdominal examination.
